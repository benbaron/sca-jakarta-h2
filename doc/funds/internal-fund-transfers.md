# Internal fund transfers

P25-S1 reallocates net assets between two unrestricted/designated funds in one company. It does not move money between bank accounts, pay another legal entity, change a donor restriction or implement NMR policy. D03 remains unresolved for entity mappings.

Choose date, distinct source/destination funds, positive amount, explanation, ordinary non-bank ASSET allocation account (DEBIT normal balance, no subtype/function) and EQUITY transfer account (CREDIT normal balance, no subtype/function). Both posting accounts belong to the active company chart. Four canonical lines: source Cr allocation / Dr net assets; destination Dr allocation / Cr net assets. The allocation account represents claims on pooled resources; its company balance nets to zero, including when a source allocation balance becomes negative. Each fund's assets and net assets change equally; company totals, bank balances and income/expense stay unchanged. No account is auto-created.

Funds and ancestors must be active on the transfer date, unrestricted/designated and without restriction text. Ledger-defined available net assets are the natural balances of EQUITY and INCOME less EXPENSE through the date, including original/reversal entries; unallocated account opening balances are not assumed to belong to a fund. Source availability must cover the amount on that date and every later recorded date, so backdating cannot consume resources already used by later entries. Serialize on the company row before availability/idempotency checks. A durable UUID request and canonical command hash make exact retries return the same transfer and reject changed/foreign-company reuse. Invalid input, closed periods, insufficient availability or persistence/audit failure rolls back the canonical transaction and FundTransfer together.

Saved transfer facts are retained. Generic direct edits/deletes are rejected; reverse through the transfer or Journal correction workflow, using an open reversal date, then create a corrected transfer. Reversal creates the opposite-direction transfer fact linked to the canonical reversal in the same database transaction; original dated history remains reportable. Reversing a reversal restores the original direction. Reversal/replacement must not create an unlinked replacement. Existing restrictions or deactivation do not erase historical reversals. Historical correction requires a reason but does not rerun new-transfer availability rules; it may reveal a negative fund balance requiring a further accounting correction.

The Funds action opens company-owned transfer entry/history. It preserves existing Fund/Journal drafts, uses saved table/divider state and scrolling, permissions, date/money formatting and hover help. New/Save/Refresh, Reverse Selected and Open Journal are real operations. Unsaved transfer changes require an intentional discard.

SCLX currently exports canonical accounting/reversal history but omits the existing FundTransfer operational master. Export must disclose that operational transfer history cannot be reconstructed on import, and recommend whole-database backup when those links are needed. P27-S4 owns full operational portability. No format or import authority is invented in P25-S1.
