1. In a disposable company copy, save an expense/check `000123` with issue date and no delivery/evidence. Open Reconciliation → Checks / Exceptions, Refresh, choose a review-through date after issue and an age cutoff covering it. Confirm amount, missing delivery/evidence and age-review exceptions. Missing issue date reports unknown age. Selecting a configured bank excludes other banks; All banks restores the full company report. No ledger action occurs by running the report.
2. Select the check, enter actual delivery, an external evidence reference, review date and a factual note confirming whether the obligation remains valid. Record Review. Confirm restart retains all facts; amounts and cleared state do not change. Review-only edits protect unsaved fields when selecting another row, refreshing or closing the panel. Cancel discard and confirm the draft remains. VIEWER can read and Refresh but cannot Review/Void/Reissue.
3. With a valid lost-check obligation, select `000123`, use an open correction date, cancellation reason and new number `000124`. Reissue Selected and confirm. Original and reversal show `000123`; the replacement shows `000124` and links back to the original. Expense totals remain one expense. Review through the day before reversal still shows the old instrument outstanding; through correction date shows the cancelled pair and the new check. Search both references in Journal. Retained linked records cannot be edited/deleted; correction uses reversal.
4. Repeat with one check allocated across two funds. Report amount is the sum; reissue preserves allocations and does not double expense. A transaction with two different check instruments rejects reissue. Duplicate new number, same number, blank reason, closed correction date, cross-company IDs and finalized/completed reconciliation reject without partial transactions or audit. A cleared/matched check requires reconciliation review before reissue.
5. Explicit cancellation without a replacement uses Void Selected and reverses the whole transaction with confirmation. Confirm its original/reversal pair in exception history and reconciliation check/reference/history columns. No pair is auto-cleared or auto-matched. If cancellation would leave a continuing obligation, do not use Void to erase it; consult accounting authority. Age review itself never creates income, reverses expense or releases an obligation. D04 stale accounting remains unresolved.
6. Review imported statement errors and unresolved amount/date/cleared-state/duplicate differences. Recorded explanations do not silently clear them. Open Reconciliation navigates to the selected difference session; resolve there using existing matching protections. Finalized history is read-only.
7. At laptop size and increased font size, horizontally scroll all history/review columns, move both vertical dividers, and shrink the review editor to expose its vertical/horizontal scrollbars. Confirm table order/width/sort and divider positions are company-owned after reopening. Dates/money follow company formatting; amounts and dates sort by their typed values.
8. Export SCLX and import into a fresh disposable company. Verify evidence/review facts, original/reversal/new references and correction links; reimport makes no duplicates. Legacy version-1 payment data remains usable with review facts unknown. External evidence files themselves are not bundled; whole-database backups retain the full audit.
