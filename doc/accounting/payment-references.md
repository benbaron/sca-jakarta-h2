# Structured payment references (P25-S2)

Payment facts belong to a canonical BANK-account split, never the transaction header. A transaction with multiple bank payments has multiple bank splits and independent method/reference/date facts. Account identity supplies the issuing-bank relationship; the optional header bank remains compatibility context. One check may allocate its bank effect across several funds: bank lines with the same account/check number in one transaction represent one instrument and must carry identical method/issue/delivery facts. Exactly one line reserves that number; the combined bank effect is the instrument amount. Distinct payments have distinct bank/reference identities.

Method is CHECK, EFT, CARD, CASH or OTHER. Reference is optional except for CHECK and is trimmed text, at most 80 characters, preserving leading zeros, case and alphanumeric identifiers. Issued and delivered dates are optional factual dates, independent of ledger date. Delivery requires an issue date and cannot precede it. Blank payment facts remain valid legacy/no-reference accounting. Memo remains narrative and is never parsed into identity. No print, delivery, void, stale or cleared state is inferred from these fields.

CHECK describes an instrument issued from an outgoing bank line, not a receipt of another party's check. Check numbers are unique within the stable issuing ledger account. Exact trimmed, case-sensitive text is compared: `000123` and `123` are different identifiers. Retained reversed originals continue reserving their number; reversal copies retain facts without reserving another instrument. EFT/card/other repeated references are permitted, since batch references can legitimately repeat. The company lock plus a unique account/key index prevent racing check creation. Direct edit may deliberately change or clear a fact and releases the prior number; before/after audit preserves it. Direct delete follows existing Settings/period/reconciliation protections and preserves its audit snapshot. Original/reversal rows remain retained under reversal policy. Reversing a reversal retains the same historical reference. Automatic reverse-and-replace rejects referenced payments; reverse separately and enter a replacement with its new reference. P25-S3 adds explicit linked check reissue and factual review; see [check exceptions](check-exceptions.md).

Canonical saves/updates validate bank ownership and write payment facts with splits and audit atomically. Closed periods and completed/finalized reconciliation protection are unchanged. Metadata does not change money, reconciliation matching or cleared state. Company boundaries and BOOKKEEPING_WRITE are enforced by the existing entry service. Journal adds editable per-line method/reference/date columns, company date formatting, dirty listeners, refreshed draft protection and a payment-bank filter. Text search includes references and bank code/name; with a payment-bank filter, reference and bank must match the same split. Returned transactions are complete. GL Detail reports the payment facts beside the issuing account, including typed CSV/PDF export.

## SCLX

Optional `extensions.scaJakartaH2.paymentReferences` version 1 contains `lines`. Each fact contains exactly `lineId`, `method`, `reference`, `issuedOn`, `deliveredOn`; optional values are JSON null, dates ISO YYYY-MM-DD. The existing line portable ID owns bank/transaction relationships. Export never writes a local account ID as portable identity. Preview validates shape/date facts and incorporates normalized facts into line and transaction conflict hashes. Atomic commit validates the resolved bank, restores correction links first, then applies facts and check reservations, with factual audit records. Selecting existing target history preserves that complete target record. Reimport is idempotent. Files without the extension remain usable, with unknown facts left absent. Bank-statement CSV/OFX references remain independent review facts; no automatic promotion into canonical payment identity.

## Migration

V79 adds nullable columns to existing txn_split and non-destructive account/reference indexes. Existing rows retain amounts/IDs and no facts are fabricated from memo. All additions are replay-safe for the existing missing-history recovery path. Database constraints cover method vocabulary, reference/delivery consistency and issuing-account duplicate reservations.


P25-S3 extends optional payment facts with evidence reference, review date and factual note, and exports version 2 while retaining version-1 import. Journal preserves these fields when editing/copying bank lines. Canonical check replacement uses a new reference and existing correction links; linked history cannot be directly edited or deleted. Age alone never changes ledger/obligation/cleared state. See [check exceptions](check-exceptions.md).
