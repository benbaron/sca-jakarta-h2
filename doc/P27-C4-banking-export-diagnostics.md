# P27-C4 readable bank-ledger export failures

The owner screenshot after merged C3 shows the bank-account ledger chart error, truncated with an ellipsis. It is not evidence of a different-company owner or of the user's precise chart IDs. C3 compares persisted chart/company IDs; a genuinely different-chart bank link still cannot be represented by the single-chart SCLX format.

The current JavaFX error Alert puts the entire diagnosis into its default content label. That label clips the account/chart context needed to review the failure. Replace it with a read-only, wrapped, selectable TextArea in a resizable dialog. Standard scrolling and Ctrl+A/C preserve the complete message, including long names. Test the real shown dialog at normal and narrow widths; do not substitute a recording dialog for this regression.

Bank-ledger chart mismatches must direct review to Banking / Bank accounts and Company Admin, rather than the Journal-entry advice appropriate to transaction lines. The owner additionally requests a detail subwindow identifying the faulty entry. The resizable failure window shows a labelled Record line: configured bank-account name and stable ID for bank configuration errors, or transaction ID/date for Journal line errors. Same-company account mismatches show the ledger account code/name/ID and separate Referenced chart and Selected chart lines with name/version/ID, distinguishing duplicate account codes. Foreign-chart labels remain suppressed; safe offending-record context and review guidance remain visible. No record is automatically relinked, no chart is switched, and no historical/reconciliation fact is omitted or rewritten. Failed export leaves the database and an existing destination unchanged.

Banking currently lists qualifying posting accounts across company-owned charts, including inactive accounts used by history. Existing configured accounts may therefore contain a different-chart ledger link even when a new Journal entry uses the correct chart. Do not assume that selecting another account merely to force export is a valid correction. Safe new-link selection and established-link/reconciliation policy are a separate candidate slice recorded in PLAN; they are not silently changed by this diagnostic correction.

Verification includes a real-H2 configured bank account referencing another owned chart with duplicate code 1000, a selected-chart inactive ledger account that still exports, database/destination preservation on failure, and the shown failure dialog's full text, selection, wrapping and scroll accessibility.

On the updated build, repeat Export Active Company to SCLX. Read or copy the entire error, including the configured bank account and both charts. Review that configuration in Banking / Bank accounts; the new Journal entry is not necessarily the offending record. Share the full details before deciding whether an established ledger link should change. This focused check does not reopen deferred native acceptance.
