# P25-S4B — Company NMR policy configuration

Owner selected this independent prerequisite on 2026-10-14: “Build policy configuration first, with no default rates or accounting entries.” This is the explicit scope amendment to the [S4A contract](P25-S4A-nmr-contract-proposal.md); full assessment/collection/remittance and P25-S3/D03 financial acceptance remain blocked.

Store dated, sourced policy versions in H2 for the active company. Each user-defined policy code/category has inclusive non-overlapping effective intervals, an explicitly entered nonnegative rate (up to four decimal places), ISO currency, date-basis/rounding descriptions, eligibility/exemption definitions, recipient/legal-reporting and merchant-path descriptions, source reference/date and authority. There are no seeded policies, default rates, default currency or implied exemption rules. Free-text rules are recorded evidence; this slice does not evaluate them or certify external approval.

Saved version terms are retained and cannot be overwritten or deleted. New creates a blank version with a fresh request identity; exact Save retries are idempotent, changed or foreign-company identity reuse rejects. End Selected Version may shorten its validity interval with an explicit reason; it cannot change the rate/source or extend an old interval. Closing an open-ended interval makes room for a later version. Both operations serialize on the company row and commit their factual before/after audit atomically. Future financial consumers must protect linked historical facts before adding their consumption workflow.

Administration -> NMR Policies exposes real New Version, Save Policy Version, Refresh and End Selected Version controls. The selected subpane publishes New/Save through the existing File/toolbar routing. Saved history is read-only; visible help explains retention. COMPANY_ADMIN (ADMIN/MANAGER) gates writes in the service and UI; authenticated users bound to the active company may read. Dirty drafts survive refresh/selection/New unless discard is explicitly confirmed. Company-owned table/divider state, typed date/rate sorting, company formatting, independent split regions and both scrolling directions apply. Display normalization must not round the authoritative four-decimal rate.

V81 is nondestructive and seeds no configuration. No Txn/TxnSplit, supplemental, fund-transfer, payment or accounting-service write is part of this slice. Whole-database backup retains configuration and audit; the later owner-selected [S4C slice](P25-S4C-nmr-policy-sclx.md) adds governed policy-only SCLX portability. Full financial NMR portability remains a later owning contract.
