## Opening and reconciliation authority

Beginning cleared balance and its date/source are prominent. Use that account's prior completed reconciliation closing statement balance, frozen as source evidence. First reconciliation requires an explicit bank opening amount/date and user confirmation that the journal opening position and outstanding items were reviewed. Do not assume zero or create a balancing/opening journal entry. Beginning book balance is separately derived from canonical journal entries before the period; bank configuration opening metadata must not be counted again as a second ledger.

Calculate ending cleared balance from the confirmed opening and current-period matched bank movements, including older outstanding journal items clearing in this period and excluding future clearings. Use actual bank posting/clearing dates. Outstanding unmatched journal checks/deposits carry forward without blocking a correct reconciliation. All eligible statement items must be accounted for, the opening must be confirmed, and difference must be exactly zero before Balanced/Complete. Zero net variance cannot hide unresolved offsetting statement rows. Completed records remain immutable. Save progress retains an unresolved session. A missing journal entry is explicitly created as a balanced canonical entry before matching; statement import alone does not post.

Match and clear is an explicit atomic operation with a reviewed bank date; normal journal/bank dates can differ. Exact company/account and equal amount are mandatory. Clearing/unmatching must affect the selected whole bank item, preserve unrelated account legs and prior evidence, and never leave partial relationships. One instrument's Fund allocations should remain together unless owner clarification selects another presentation; distinct instruments must not collapse. Confirmed bank facts remain on canonical splits and existing reconciliation relationships.

## How to use

Open Banking and select a bank/account. Accounts shows the full linked ledger/chart identity and configuration status. Bank journal lists the bank portion of canonical journal entries and permits reference edits when the underlying entry is editable. Reconcile selects the saved statement for that account or starts a new statement. Import OFX/CSV or enter the statement header/lines manually. For the first statement, explicitly confirm the opening date/balance and reviewed starting position. Match equal amounts, review outstanding items and the difference, Save progress or Complete when balanced. Later statements inherit that account's preceding completed closing balance. Unmatch restores every allocation's prior clearing evidence; transfers remain separate per bank account.
