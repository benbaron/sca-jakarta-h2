Status: VERIFYING; final focused/JavaFX passed, full local and exact published-head CI pending. Owner review/merge remain. Extends [S4G](P25-S4G-nmr-settlement-evidence.md) under [S4A](P25-S4A-nmr-contract-proposal.md). PLAN owns execution evidence.

## Contract

Reuse existing RECEIVABLE allocations and dated lifecycle authority. Users optionally designate GROSS_RECEIPT (INCREASE) or SETTLEMENT (DECREASE); the linked receivable must be debit-normal ASSET/RECEIVABLE with company Merchant and Event. Designated item allocations retain Merchant/account/fund/event consistently, including corrections. Unclassified older allocations remain readable and visibly incomplete; designation never infers a rate, fee or settlement.

Each SETTLEMENT may explicitly link zero or more whole canonical debit splits as PAYOUT (BANK), FEE (EXPENSE), or NMR_DISCHARGE (credit-normal OTHER_LIABILITY, fully allocated to existing evidenced MERCHANT_DISCHARGE facts). Components must belong to the same transaction, Merchant, Fund and Event as the receivable allocation. No component may be claimed twice, including across items, and their total cannot exceed that allocation. Split a shared payout/fee into explicit item lines when allocating multiple items. Unexplained offsets remain visible; no difference is automatically called a fee or NMR. Links carry no copied money or independent balance.

Optional withheld-unconfirmed amount is a nonnegative four-decimal **observation at that allocation's transaction date**, with a nonblank external evidence pointer (maximum 500 characters). Blank is unknown; explicit zero is an entered observation. It is not a receivable application or liability discharge, is not added across observations and is never subtracted from the equation. The report shows dated observations and the latest uncancelled recorded observation with its original date; this is not a separately calculated outstanding-withholding balance or proof of external settlement. A later observation can explicitly record zero. Observations are saved with actual receipt/settlement accounting, not through a standalone posting or attachment workflow.

Journal Receivables exposes role, observed withholding/evidence and comma-separated 1-based payout/fee/NMR component line references. Blank drafts stay blank; Apply Existing Item does not guess new facts. Existing Save Entry/File Save, dirty protection, permissions, company ownership, period/reconciliation, Delete/Reverse/correction, atomic audit and rollback govern all metadata. The touched Receivables controls/table gain the existing real remembered divider and independent two-axis scrolling pattern.

**Merchant Receivable Movements** uses requested Start/End and existing dated effects: opening + gross receipts − net payouts − fees − confirmed NMR discharge + unexplained change = closing receivable. Net component columns include their own dated reversals; corrections are informational and already included. Unknown gross movements and GL/detail coverage remain explicit; unknown increases/reductions cannot cancel into a complete classification. Totals/control coverage remain full despite disclosed detail limits. Read-only Generate/Export and transaction drill-through reuse Report Library and company formatting. All receivable items are visible, including unclassified ones; Merchant is explicit, not inferred from free-text counterparties.

SCLX supplemental detail optionally carries strict version-1 `merchantSettlement` with role, withheldUnconfirmed (exact decimal text or null), evidenceReference (text or null), and components (role/portable lineId pairs). References resolve only within the owning transaction. Facts participate in complete-transaction/detail conflicts; absence preserves compatible legacy hashes. Malformed shapes/versions/precision/kinds/effects/components/ownership block Preview/Save. Existing Keep target, exact retry, canonical atomic history import and inverse linkage govern portability. No optional policy record is required.
